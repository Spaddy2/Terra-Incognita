Explorer's Handbook

Goal

Chart the unknown continent. Recover 3 map fragments from ruins and temples to reveal the Lost City — then reach it before your supplies run out.

Controls

  • WASD / Arrows — travel
  • Click — auto-travel to a charted tile
  • C — camp until morning
  • M — expedition map   H — help
  • Mouse wheel — zoom

Terrain

  • Grasslands & beaches — easy going
  • Forests, deserts, hills — slower, costlier
  • Swamps & peaks — grueling
  • Water — impassable

Survival

  • Every step burns supplies; harsh terrain burns more
  • Forage as you travel — forests are generous
  • Villages ⛺ fully resupply your expedition
  • Night narrows your vision; rain slows you down
  • Obelisks 🗼 chart the land around them
